# Role Curriculum Matrix

Curriculum is generated from **role + effective route permissions**, not a fixed menu.

| Role | Training emphasis |
|---|---|
| SA | Whole-business oversight, administration, workflows, reports, users and training governance |
| AD | System administration, users/RBAC, masters, operational oversight and training governance |
| OP | Booking/order, vehicle planning, Smart Load, LR, dispatch, delivery/POD and operational exceptions |
| BU | Branch bookings, order/LR execution, POD and branch work queues |
| AC | Billing, receivables, customer payments, approvals, detention and finance controls |
| CC | Customer/LR visibility, delivery follow-up, POD and customer communication |
| CO | Fleet, drivers, trips, fuel/expenses, trip completion and operational vehicle work |
| SI | Workshop, job cards, spares, purchasing, inward/replacement and service bills/payments |
| HR | Employees, attendance/leaves/shifts, HR masters and payroll workflows |

Per-user extra permissions add relevant training; denied permissions remove inaccessible screen training. Shared Getting Started content is de-duplicated.
